### what: 
a shared repository for the devices team in the [musical-connections](https://engineering.nyu.edu/research/student-research/vertically-integrated-projects/vip-teams/musical-connections) research group at nyu.

we are working with the nordoff-robbins center for music therapy, helping them design custom instruments for people to express themselves sonically in music-therapy sessions.
